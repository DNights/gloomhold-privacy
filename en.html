Gloomhold Privacy Policy

Effective: October 23, 2026 · App: Gloomhold (package dev.game.gloomhold) · 한국어

1. Summary

Gloomhold does not collect any personal information. There is no sign-up, no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting tools, and nothing is sent to any server run by the developer.

2. Information stored only on your device

Game progress (save files), settings (such as volume and language), your co-op nickname and a random device key used to rejoin co-op games are stored only on your device. The developer cannot access them, and they are removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Co-op (playing together)

Co-op connects devices directly on the same network (Wi-Fi). An invite code contains the local network address of the device that opened the room. While you play together, your nickname, the random device key and the information needed to run the game (positions, actions, etc.) are exchanged only between the devices playing together; nothing is sent to or stored by the developer or any third party. Your nickname is visible to the other players.

4. Permissions

The Internet and network-state permissions are used only for co-op connections. The app does not request any other permission related to personal information, such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage.

5. Children

This app does not collect personal information from users of any age.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If server features or any data collection are added, this policy will be updated beforehand.
