Gloomhold Privacy Policy

Effective: November 28, 2026 · App: Gloomhold (package dev.game.gloomhold) · 한국어

1. Summary

There is no sign-up, and the developer does not receive personal information such as your name, email address or contact details. The app uses no advertising or crash-reporting tools, and the developer runs no server. Only if you agree, play records used to improve the game (floors reached, causes of death, etc.) are sent to Google Analytics (Firebase) (section 5). The game is the same if you decline. Also, if you choose to sign in to Google Play Games, your achievements, leaderboard scores and game saves (cloud save) are stored by Google (section 4).

2. Information stored only on your device

Game progress (save files), settings (such as volume), your co-op nickname, and a random device key used to rejoin co-op games are stored only on your device. The developer cannot access this information, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app.

3. Co-op

Co-op connects devices directly on the same network (Wi-Fi). An invite code contains the local network address of the device that opened the room. While playing together, your nickname, the random device key, and information needed for gameplay (position, actions, etc.) are exchanged only between the devices playing together, and are not sent to or stored by the developer or any third party. Your nickname is visible to the people you play with.

4. Google Play Games (optional)

Signing in to Play Games is optional; every feature of the game works without it. If you sign in, Google processes and stores your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar) and, for this game, which achievements you have unlocked and your leaderboard scores (number of boss clears, and the number of days it took to defeat your first boss), and your game saves (game progress in your solo and co-op host save slots, saved to the cloud so you can continue on another device). Players who join someone else's co-op game have no save, so nothing is uploaded for them. Leaderboards may show your Play Games profile and score to other Play Games users.

This information is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. In the Google Play Console the developer can see only aggregate achievement and leaderboard statistics and the public leaderboards, and does not receive your email address or other Google account information. You can delete this game's Play Games data in the Play Games settings.

5. Play data collection (only if you agree)

The game asks on first launch and sends data only if you agree. Every feature works the same if you decline. You can agree or withdraw at any time in Settings → Privacy → Send play data.

  • What is sent: in-game records (floors reached, the floor and cause of death (such as the monster type), the floor you escaped from, bosses defeated, types of base facilities built, cycle), app usage (first launch, number of sessions and play time), and device and app information (app version, device model, OS version, language, approximate country/region, and where the app was installed from).
  • What is not sent: your name, email, contact details, advertising ID, Google account, nickname or precise location. Records are linked only by a random identifier that Firebase creates for each installation (app instance ID), and the developer cannot identify you from it.
  • Purpose: to see statistics such as which floors players find hard and how long they keep playing, in order to improve the game's difficulty and content. It is not used for advertising or marketing, and it is not sold.
  • Who processes it (processor, international transfer): Google LLC (Firebase, Google Analytics). After you agree, data is sent over the internet from time to time while you play and is stored on Google servers, including in the United States. It is handled under the Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Retention: deleted automatically 14 months after it is received.
  • Withdrawing: once you turn it off in Settings, nothing more is sent and the analytics identifier on your device is reset so that new records are not linked to earlier ones.

6. How to delete your data

App: Gloomhold · Developer: DNights

  • Play Games records (achievements, leaderboard scores, cloud save, gamer profile): these are kept by Google. In the Google Play Games app, go to your profile → Settings to delete this game's (Gloomhold's) data or your whole Play Games account (also possible from your Google Account). Deletion and retention follow the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Information on your device (save files, settings, nickname, device key): deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or go to Android Settings → Apps → Gloomhold → Storage → Clear data. No copy is kept by the developer.
  • Play data (section 5): withdrawing in Settings stops sending and resets the analytics identifier on your device. Records already sent are deleted automatically after 14 months. If you want them deleted sooner, contact the address below. Because the developer cannot identify individual players, the request will be handled in whatever way is possible (such as requesting deletion of records for the relevant period).
  • Apart from the play data above, the developer keeps no user data.

7. Sharing records

When you share a boss-clear record, the record image and text are handed to the app you pick (a messenger, social network, etc.). You decide what to post and where; nothing is sent to the developer.

8. Permissions

The internet and network-state permissions are used only for co-op connections, Play Games (if you sign in) and sending play data (if you agree). The advertising ID permission has been removed from the app. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage or any other personal-data permission.

9. Children

The developer does not receive personal information such as names or contact details from users of any age. Play data (section 5) is sent only if you agree; users under 14 should decide together with a parent or guardian.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new effective date. If server or data-collection features are added, this policy will be updated beforehand.
